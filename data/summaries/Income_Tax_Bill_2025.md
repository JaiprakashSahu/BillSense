# Summary: Income Tax Bill 2025

# Income-Tax Bill, 2025: Comprehensive Plain-Language Summary

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Income-Tax Bill, 2025** is a comprehensive consolidation of all existing Indian income tax laws, replacing the Income-Tax Act, 1961. It comes into effect on **April 1, 2026**. This new Act simplifies and modernizes the complex framework of how individuals, companies, and other entities calculate, declare, and pay taxes in India. It updates definitions to reflect modern economic activities, such as digital assets, e-commerce, and specialized sectors like power generation and mining.

The Bill introduces significant updates to tax incentives, penalties, and administrative procedures. Notable changes include a strict **60% tax on specific intangible income** (like patent licensing), updated presumptive taxation rates for non-resident shipping and aviation, and enhanced digital compliance requirements through mandatory **PAN and Aadhaar integration**. The legislation aims to improve transparency, curb tax evasion through robust search and seizure powers (including for digital assets), and streamline the appeal process for taxpayers.

## 2. Key Provisions

*   **Effective Date & Scope:** The entire framework operates from April 1, 2026, consolidating previous tax laws (Sec. 1).
*   **Mandatory Digital Identification:** PAN is mandatory for businesses with turnover >₹5 lakh or transactions >₹2.5 lakh. **Aadhaar linkage** is compulsory for eligible individuals; failure to link makes the PAN inoperative (Sec. 262).
*   **Strict Taxation on Intangibles:** Income from patent transfers, licensing, and technical knowledge is taxed at a flat **60% on gross amounts**, with no deductions allowed (Secs. 105, 195).
*   **Presumptive Taxation for Non-Residents:** Specific flat rates replace complex accounting for certain sectors:
    *   Ships (Non-cruise): 7.5%
    *   Cruise Ships: 20%
    *   Aircraft Operations: 5%
    *   Turnkey Power/Mineral Oil Services: 10%
    *   Electronics Manufacturing: 25% (Sec. 61).
*   **Capital Gains Updates:**
    *   Short-term gains on listed equities taxed at **20%**.
    *   Long-term gains on most assets taxed at **12.5%** (Secs. 196–197).
    *   Exemption on house property sales capped at **₹10 Crores**, with clawback provisions if funds are misused (Sec. 81).
*   **Deduction Limits:**
    *   Employer contributions to provident/superannuation funds capped at **14% of salary** (Sec. 29).
    *   Interest on savings deposits deductible up to ₹10,000 (general citizens) or ₹50,000 (senior citizens) (Sec. 153).
*   **Investor Protection Funds:** Tax exemption for infrastructure funds is subject to a "recapture rule" if untaxed credit is shared with insiders (Secs. 28–30).
*   **Agro & Oil/Gas Incentives:** Deductions available for deposits in welfare funds for Tea/Coffee/Rubber (up to 40% of profits) and Oil/Gas (up to 20% of profits), with strict withdrawal restrictions (Sec. 49, Sch. X).

## 3. Rights & Obligations

### Rights
*   **Advance Rulings:** Taxpayers (resident and non-resident) can apply for binding Advance Rulings on tax matters, with proceedings suspended during the pendency of the application (Secs. 380–389).
*   **Rebates:** Residents earning ≤₹5 lakh receive a 100% tax rebate (max ₹12,500). Specific income earners ≤₹12 lakh get a 100% rebate (max ₹60,000) (Secs. 155–156).
*   **Disability Allowance:** Certified disabled individuals can claim deductions of ₹75,000 (general) or ₹1,25,000 (severe disabilities) (Sec. 154).
*   **Industrial Relocation:** Deferral of capital gains tax for industrial units moving from urban to non-urban areas upon reinvestment of proceeds (Sec. 87).

### Obligations
*   **Filing Returns:** Individuals above exemption limits and businesses must file returns. Specific audit reports are required for non-residents in presumptive taxation sectors (Secs. 63–66, 262A).
*   **Transfer Pricing Compliance:** Companies with international transactions must file specific statements. Non-filing incurs daily penalties (Sec. 458).
*   **TDS/TCS Compliance:** Deductors must withhold tax and comply with expanded TCS scopes (e.g., motor vehicles, overseas tours, e-commerce).
*   **Registration Renewal:** Charitable trusts and public utility bodies must maintain registration. Small entities (income <₹5 crore) get 10-year validity; others get 3–5 years (Secs. 335–356).
*   **Digital Asset Disclosure:** Taxpayers must maintain books for virtual digital spaces and submitted audited reports where applicable (Sec. 261).

## 4. Penalties & Enforcement

*   **Non-Compliance Fines:**
    *   Failure to audit/report: Lesser of 0.5% of turnover or ₹1.5 Lakh (Sec. 446).
    *   TDS failures: Penalty equals the tax liability itself (Secs. 448–449).
    *   Inaccurate Transfer Pricing info: ₹50,000 or fixed ₹5 Lakh for funds (Secs. 454–456).
    *   Misuse of Welfare Funds: Withdrawals for prohibited purposes are immediately taxable as business profits (Sch. X).
*   **Criminal Offenses:**
    *   Jurisdiction lies with Judicial Magistrates of the first class or higher (Sec. 520).
    *   Leniency acts like the *Probation of Offenders Act* are excluded, except for those under 18 (Sec. 521>.
*   **Search & Seizure Powers:**
    *   Authorities can seize "assets" including **bullion, jewellery, and digital storage devices** (Sec. 261).
    *   Presumption of ownership: Items found in control are presumed to belong to the possessor (Sec. 524).
    *   Joint searches do not create a legal "association of persons," but assessments remain individual (Sec. 525).
*   **Time Limits:** Reassessment proceedings have strict timelines (e.g., 6 months standard, extendable to 9; +12 months if referred to TP Officer) (Sec. 359 et al.).

## 5. Notable Exceptions or Limitations

*   **Exclusions from Tax:**
    *   Agricultural income (defined in Sec. 2).
    *   Income from specific charitable purposes, explicitly including **yoga** (Sec. 2).
    *   Interest/capital gains from approved Provident Funds (Sec. 32).
    *   Income from CG-notified international sporting events involving >2 countries (Sec. 33).
*   **Exemptions from Offense Provisions:** The *Probation of Offenders Act* does not apply to tax offenses (except minors under 18) (Sec. 521).
*   **Clawback Rules:** Capital gains exemptions on house property are reversed if a new income-generating house is purchased/built within 2–3 years, or the old asset is sold again within 3 years (Sec. 81).
*   **Limitations on Deductions:**
    *   Expenses must be "wholly and exclusively" for business; personal use disallows full deduction (Sec. 28).
    *   Deductions for Head Office expenditure are capped at 5% of adjusted total income (Sec. 60).
    *   Total Chapter IX deductions cannot exceed the total tax liability (Sec. 155).
*   **Digital Asset Limitations:** "Personal effects" exclusions do not cover jewelry or art; these are treated as property for tax purposes (Sec. 2).

## 6. Key Definitions

*   **Agricultural Income:** Defined within the scope of Sec. 2, excluding it from general taxability.
*   **Amalgamation:** Transfer of property/liabilities of one company to another, where shareholders of the transferor hold at least **75% of the transfer company's share value** (Sec. 2).
*   **Company in which Public are Substantially Interested:** Includes entities with **≥40% government holding** or **≥50% public beneficial ownership** (Sec. 2).
*   **Charitable Purpose:** Explicitly includes **yoga** (Sec. 2).
*   **E-commerce:** Distinguishes between the "supply" (digital product), the "operator" (platform), and the "participant" (resident seller) (Sec. 2).
*   **Virtual Digital Space:** Encompasses email, social media, and banking systems, bringing them within the scope of search/seizure "computer systems" (Sec. 261).
*   **Foreign Exchange Assets:** Assets acquired with convertible foreign exchange, relevant for NRI taxation of investment income and long-term capital gains (Sec. 212).
*   **Goodwill:** Explicitly excluded from the definition of "assets" for certain deduction contexts (Sec. 2).
*   **Consideration for Immovable Property:** Broadened to include maintenance fees and club fees, relevant for TCS purposes (Sec. 2).
