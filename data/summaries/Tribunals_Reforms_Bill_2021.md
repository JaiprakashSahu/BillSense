# Summary: Tribunals Reforms Bill 2021

### 1. Executive Summary

The Tribunals Reforms Act, 2021, is a legislative framework designed to overhaul the administration, appointment, and functioning of quasi-judicial bodies in India. Its primary goal is to standardize the service conditions of tribunal officials and create a transparent, merit-based process for selecting judges and members. By establishing specific Search-cum-Selection Committees (SCCs) chaired by senior judicial figures, the bill aims to reduce political interference and ensure that those making legal determinations are qualified and impartial. This reform is critical for restoring public confidence in the efficiency and fairness of regulatory and administrative adjudication.

**Key outcomes of the bill include:**
*   A unified tenure and age limit structure for all tribunal officials, removing inconsistent terms previously found in various individual acts.
*   A streamlined process for the Intellectual Property Appellate Board (IPAB), clarifying its jurisdiction over patents, trademarks, and copyrights.
*   Strict safeguards to protect tribunal officials from arbitrary removal or disadvantageous changes to their salary and service conditions once appointed.

The bill matters because it addresses long-standing concerns about the independence and accountability of India’s tribunal system. By clearly defining who can be appointed, for how long, and under what conditions, it seeks to create a stable and predictable legal environment for businesses and citizens who rely on these bodies for dispute resolution.

### 2. Key Provisions

*   **Standardized Appointment Criteria:** Applicants for tribunal positions must be at least 50 years old. Selections are made by a Search-cum-Selection Committee (SCC) rather than by direct government choice. (Sec. 3)
*   **Composition of Selection Committees:**
    *   **Central Tribunals:** The SCC is chaired by the Chief Justice of India (or their nominee). It includes two Secretaries to the Government, the outgoing or sitting Chairperson, and the relevant Ministry Secretary (non-voting). The Chair has a casting vote to break ties. (Sec. 3(3))
    *   **State Tribunals:** The SCC is chaired by the state’s Chief Justice. It includes the State Chief Secretary, the State Public Service Commission Chairman, the outgoing/sitting Chairperson, and the State Administrative Secretary (non-voting). (Sec. 3(3) proviso)
*   **Unified Tenure and Age Limits:**
    *   **Chairpersons:** Serve a 4-year term or until the age of 70. (Sec. 5 & 183)
    *   **Members:** Serve a 4-year term or until the age of 67. (Sec. 5 & 183)
    *   *Note:* These limits override existing laws for appointees made on or after the bill’s effective date.
*   **Intellectual Property Appellate Board (IPAB) Structure:** The Ipab consists of a Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, and Members. Benches for hearing cases must include both a Judicial Member and a Technical Member. (Ss. 84, 85)
    *   **Patent Experts:** Technical members specializing in patents must have served as the Controller of Patents for 5 years or as a Registered Patent Agent for 10 years. (Ss. 116, 117)
*   **Protection of Service Conditions:** The Central Government can set rules for remuneration and removal. However, once an official is appointed, their salary, allowances, or other service conditions cannot be altered in a way that is disadvantageous to them. (Sec. 184)
*   **Trademark Proceedings:** The Registrar may allow legal successors to step in for deceased parties, or allow proceedings to continue through representatives to ensure continuity. (Sec. 130)

### 3. Rights & Obligations

**Rights of Applicants and Officials:**
*   **Fair Selection:** Applicants have the right to be considered by an independent committee that includes judicial leadership, rather than being subject to arbitrary political appointment.
*   **Job Security:** Once appointed, Chairpersons and Members have the right to a fixed tenure (4 years) and cannot be removed arbitrarily. They are also protected from negative changes to their pay or benefits during their term.

**Obligations of Individuals (Applicants and Members):**
*   **Age Requirement:** Applicants must be at least 50 years old.
*   **Conflicts of Interest:** Former members of the IPAB are prohibited from appearing before the Board or the Registrar in any capacity after their term ends. (Sec. 94)
*   **Qualifications:** Technical members (especially in patents) must meet specific experience thresholds (e.g., 10 years as a Registered Patent Agent).

**Obligations of the Government:**
*   **Constitution of Committees:** The Central and State Governments must ensure the SCCs are formed correctly with the prescribed judicial and administrative members.
*   **Rule-Making:** The Central Government is responsible for prescribing detailed rules regarding qualifications, appointment procedures, remuneration, and removal processes.

### 4. Penalties & Enforcement

The provided summaries focus primarily on **structural reforms, appointment procedures, and service conditions** rather than criminal penalties for violations. However, the following mechanisms serve as enforcement and accountability tools:

*   **Ineligibility as a Penalty:** If a candidate does not meet the strict eligibility criteria (minimum age of 50, specific experience for technical roles), they are barred from appointment. This acts as a preventive enforcement of the qualification standards.
*   **Post-Term Restrictions:** For IPAB members, the prohibition on appearing before the Board post-term (Sec. 94) is a strict legal limit. Violating this would likely render any proceedings involving such individuals void or subject to legal challenge, as it compromises the integrity of the tribunal.
*   **Tenure Violations:** Appointing someone beyond the statutory age limits (70 for Chairpersons, 67 for Members) or extending tenure beyond the 4-year maximum would violate the Act (Sec. 5, Sec. 183). Such appointments would be considered legally invalid.

*Note: Specific monetary fines or imprisonment terms for procedural violations are not detailed in the provided excerpts.*

### 5. Notable Exceptions or Limitations

*   **Transitional Provisions for Existing Appointees:** The new tenure and age limits (4 years/70 or 67) generally apply to appointees made *on or after* the designated notification date. However, there are specific transitional exceptions for those who were already appointed between **May 26, 2017, and the notification date**, who may retain terms from their original governing act. (Sec. 5, Sec. 183)
*   **Existing Incumbents:** Those already in office before the designated date may continue to serve under the terms of the laws under which they were originally appointed, rather than being immediately subjected to the new unified conditions. (Sec. 183)
*   **Non-Voting Members:** In the selection committees (SCCs), the Ministry Secretary (for Central) and the State Administrative Secretary (for State) have a non-voting role. Their participation is limited to providing administrative context, ensuring they do not directly influence the selection outcome through a vote. (Sec. 3(3))

### 6. Key Definitions

*   **Chairperson:** The head of a tribunal, eligible until age 70, serving a 4-year term. Selected via the SCC chaired by the Chief Justice (Central) or High Court Chief Justice (State).
*   **Member:** A judicial or technical expert serving on a tribunal bench, eligible until age 67, serving a 4-year term.
*   **Search-cum-Selection Committee (SCC):** The body responsible for recommending candidates for tribunal positions. It is a hybrid of judicial, senior administrative, and functional experts.
*   **Technical Member (IPAB):** A specific role within the Intellectual Property Appellate Board who must have specialized experience (e.g., as a Patent Controller or Registered Agent) to provide expert opinion alongside a Judicial Member.
*   **Casting Vote:** The power held by the Chairperson of the SCC to break a tie in the committee’s decision-making process, ensuring a selection can be made even if the committee is evenly divided.
